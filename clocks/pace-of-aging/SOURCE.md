Analysis code for the "pace of aging" clock combining standard labs (HbA1c,
CRP, hemoglobin) and physical parameters (grip strength, lung function,
balance, gait speed, blood pressure, waist circumference). The derived
HRSPoA measure is also distributed as a public data product via the
[HRS data portal](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/pace-aging-hrs-hrspoa).
