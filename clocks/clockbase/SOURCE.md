- **Paper**: Ying, K. et al. "Autonomous AI agents discover aging interventions
  from millions of molecular profiles." Preprint at *bioRxiv* (2023, updated
  through 2025/2026). https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.02.28.530532

An autonomous agentic AI system that analyzes public aging methylation/RNA-seq
datasets across species with 40 clock predictions, rather than a single clock
implementation. Also has a live public app at https://www.clockbase.org/.
This is a large, more application-like codebase compared to the other
single-purpose clock packages in this directory - expect more setup work to
run standalone.
