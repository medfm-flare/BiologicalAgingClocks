Multi-clock R package. Vendored here primarily as the implementation source for:

- **"Intrinsic capacity" clock** - Fuentealba, M. et al. "A blood-based epigenetic
  clock for intrinsic capacity predicts mortality and is associated with clinical,
  immunological and lifestyle factors." *Nat. Aging* 5, 1207–1216 (2025).
  No dedicated repo from the original authors; the 91-probe weights have been
  incorporated into this community package.

Also includes implementations of Horvath, Horvath2, Hannum, DNAmTL, and others.

Direct copy at the commit above, git history stripped. To update, re-clone
upstream at a newer commit and replace this directory.
